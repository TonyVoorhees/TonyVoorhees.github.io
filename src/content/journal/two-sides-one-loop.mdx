<p class="text-prose leading-prose m-5">
  I made some hats.
</p>

<p class="text-prose leading-prose m-5">
  Not a launch — just a trial run on some proofs: a denim bucket hat, a snapback, a black five-panel, all carrying the OO mark I’ve started using everywhere else in the studio. Two circles, slightly slanted, small enough that they ask a question before they answer one.
</p>

<p class="text-prose leading-prose m-5">
  Eyes, maybe. Wheels in motion. An infinity symbol. Whichever you see first.
</p>

<p class="text-prose leading-prose m-5">
  That ambiguity is not a flaw in the mark. It is the mark doing what I want it to do.
</p>

<p class="text-prose leading-prose m-5">
  The bucket hat has a frayed brim. The snapback carries the embroidery slightly off-center. The classic five-panel is black, with the mark in pale thread: small variations, same gesture.
</p>

<p class="text-prose leading-prose m-5">
  The words came later: Two sides, one loop. 
</p>

<p class="text-prose leading-prose m-5">
  First real studio merch. No shop yet — if you want one, let me know.
</p>
