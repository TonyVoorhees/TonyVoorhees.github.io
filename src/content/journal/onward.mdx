</p>
<p class="font-serif font-350 text-[clamp(1.5rem,2.2vw,2rem)] leading-[1.4] m-0 dropcap tracking-tight">
  Two circles, one shared wall — product on one side, brand on the other, closer together than most people build them. It replaces my “TV” mark, which I’ve used for the past 10 years.
</p>


<p class="text-prose leading-prose m-0">
  Consider it the next marker on a trail I’ve been on for a long time, even before I had a name for where it was leading.
</p>

<p class="text-prose leading-prose mb-5">
  This isn’t the summit. It’s another point along the way — a chance to look back at how far I’ve come, take in the view, and keep moving.
</p>

<Marginalia text="Where it stands" mt={56}>
  <p class="text-prose leading-prose mb-5">

  </p>
</Marginalia>

<p class="text-prose leading-prose mb-5">
