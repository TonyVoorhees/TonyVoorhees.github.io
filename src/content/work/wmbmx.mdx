      The<span class="italic"> Idea</span>
    </h2>
    <div class="prose">
      <p class="font-serif italic font-light text-2xl"><span class="font-mono text-accent"> → </span>The brand needed to feel built, not manufactured.</p>
      <p>Wes came to the project with a typeface he wanted to use for the name. I reworked it into a custom typemark, turning the two O's in "Moore" into mag wheels — a literal detail that ties the identity back to the bikes themselves. The wheel repeats in the standalone badge mark, wrapped in a ring reading "Custom BMX Builds · WesMooreBMX.com."</p>
      <p>From there, I built out two color systems on the same mark: a warm gradient running red to orange to yellow, and a neutral gradient running white to gray to black. The typemark holds up in both directions — a brand meant to wrap around builds that range from bold and loud to something quieter.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

      <p>The website is built around the same idea as the business itself: share a theme, collaborate on the details, build something personal. It walks customers through four stages — share a theme, collaborate on the design, build the bike, get it delivered — turning what could be an intimidating custom process into something approachable. I wrote the copy myself, keeping the same direct, unpolished voice throughout the site that the brand is built around.</p>
