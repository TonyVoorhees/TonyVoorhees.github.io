  <div class="mx-auto prose max-w-(--container-prose)">
    <h2>The Problem</h2>
    <p>EVgo's website didn't match its market position. Marketing and product had been building in silos for years — duplicate navigations, conflicting styles, and every form or landing page requiring engineering time just to ship. Load times ran past 3 seconds. Bounce rate sat at 72%. Basic accessibility — alt tags, color contrast, keyboard tabbing — was failing.</p>
    <p>None of that was a design problem in isolation. It was a coordination problem wearing a design problem's clothes: two teams optimizing for different goals, with no shared system to work from.</p>
  </div>
</section>

The mandate: unify the experience, fix the infrastructure underneath it, and get marketing out from under engineering's queue.</p>

    <h2>How I Worked</h2>
    <p>Weekly Scrum sprints, daily standups, Friday demos, design work running parallel to dev cycles. I worked across six-plus departments — Customer Acquisition, Operations, Partnerships, Product, Analytics, QA — and managed 20+ marketing stakeholders across B2C, B2B, and partnership funnels.
