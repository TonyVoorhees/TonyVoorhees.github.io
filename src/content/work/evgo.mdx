  <div class="mx-auto prose max-w-(--container-prose)">
    <h2>The Insight</h2>
    <p>The experience needed to behave like one product.</p>
    <p>A driver doesn't think in terms of "website," "app," "charging network," or "account management." They think: Where can I charge? Can I get there? How much will it cost? Can I start charging? What happens next?</p>
    <p>That meant the design system couldn't just make individual screens consistent. It needed to make the experience itself feel connected.</p>
  </div>
</section>
