I want everyone to be able to read my work and get in touch, regardless of how they browse. Accessibility is treated as part of the craft here, not an afterthought.

## Conformance

This site aims to meet **WCAG 2.1 Level AA**. I test with keyboard navigation, screen-reader spot checks, and automated audits as part of every release.

## What I've done

- **Semantic structure** — real headings, landmarks, and lists, so assistive technology can navigate the page.
- **Keyboard support** — every interactive element is reachable and operable without a mouse, with a visible focus state.
- **Color contrast** — text meets AA contrast against its background in both the light and dark themes.
- **Reduced motion** — scroll reveals and transitions are disabled for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
- **Responsive text** — layouts reflow cleanly and stay usable when text is zoomed.

## Known limitations

No site is ever perfectly accessible. If you hit a barrier I've missed, I want to know.
